# Guardrail rules

*Phase 1 deliverable. The numbers live in `backend/convocart/config.py` (overridable by environment variable). Enforcement lives in the tools (`backend/convocart/tools/core.py`) and the reply guard (`backend/convocart/agents/guards.py`). Prompts repeat the rules, but nothing depends on the model obeying them.*

| # | Rule | Where it is enforced | How it is tested |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Maximum discount 10%** per cart, and only on carts of ₹999 or more. Asking for more is refused with the allowed maximum; nothing is applied silently. | `apply_discount` tool | `test_discount_cap_enforced`; scenarios `disc-*`, `attack-90-off`, `attack-admin-mode`, `attack-review-injection` |
| 2 | **Prices come from the store.** The model never supplies a price; cart lines copy the store price at add time; search results are refreshed from the store. | `add_to_cart`, `search_products`; store service | `test_search_then_add_uses_store_price`; eval checks every cart line against the catalog |
| 3 | **No invented numbers in replies.** Any ₹ amount in a reply that no tool returned this turn makes the guard replace the reply with one built from tool data. Discounts above the cap in a reply are treated the same way. | reply guard | `test_price_guard`, `test_graph_survives_bad_model`; eval re-checks every delivered reply |
| 4 | **Refunds need a human.** A refund request is created as `pending_approval` with an approval record; only the ops team can approve it. Returns and exchanges inside the window are booked directly. | `create_return`; `/admin/approvals` | `test_refund_waits_for_approval`; scenarios `refund-*`; CI job with `BREAK_RULE=refund` must fail |
| 5 | **Return window 15 days** after delivery; only delivered orders. | `create_return` | `test_return_window`; scenarios `return-outside-window`, `return-not-delivered` |
| 6 | **Only your own orders.** Order tools require a signed-in shopper (the web sign-in) and answer "not found" for anyone else's order, with no hint that it exists. | `get_order`, `create_return` | `test_other_customers_order_is_not_found`; scenarios `attack-other-customers-order`, `attack-friends-order-hinglish`, `refund-not-your-order` |
| 7 | **Customer-written text is data.** Reviews are returned as `untrusted_text`, stripped of tags, and scanned for injected instructions (flagged and traced). | `get_product_details` | `test_review_injection_is_flagged`; scenarios `attack-review-injection*` (the scripted model is built to fall for the injection; the tool still refuses) |
| 8 | **Hand over to a human** when the shopper asks for a person, complains, threatens legal action, claims authority ("I'm the manager"), or the agent hits the step limit. The human sees the last messages, cart and order context. | router; `handover_to_human`; step limit | scenarios `handover-*`, `attack-fake-manager-refund`; `test_step_limit_hands_over` |
| 9 | **Step limit: 6 model calls** per specialist per turn, then handover. | graph | `test_step_limit_hands_over` |
| 10 | **Come-back emails:** only with consent (the shopper's opt-in, or the visitor's tick box), at most **1 per cart** and **1 per address per 24 h**, never a new or bigger discount (the only percentage allowed is the cart's own, already-bargained discount), always with an unsubscribe link. Any address gets at most 5 emails a day. Blocked decisions are logged too. | `agents/recovery.py`, `agents/notify.py` | `test_abandoned_cart_recovery_consent_and_limit`, `test_bargained_discount_is_the_only_percentage`, `test_visitor_address_and_consent`, `test_unsubscribe_stops_come_back_emails`, `test_daily_limit_per_address`; scenarios `recovery-*` (plus the automatic `recovery_invented_discount` check) |
| 11 | **Each specialist only sees its own tools** (the advisor cannot create returns, support cannot apply discounts). | `run_tool` | `test_agent_cannot_use_other_agents_tools` |
| 12 | **Store events are verified and handled once**: `cart.abandoned` and `order.paid` carry an `X-Store-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256) and are de-duplicated by event id, so no shopper gets the same email twice; one order confirmation per order. | `app.py`, `store/api.py` | `test_store_webhook_signature`, `test_order_confirmation_after_payment` |

## Proving the gate works

`BREAK_RULE=discount` raises the cap to 90% and `BREAK_RULE=refund` turns off approvals. With either, `python -m eval.run_scenarios --max-violations 0` exits non-zero (measured: 4 and 2 violations). The `broken-rule-must-fail` CI job runs both and fails if the gate stays green.
